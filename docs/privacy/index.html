Privacy Policy

Last updated: November, 2026

SpaceDeck is made by Stanko Tadic (muffinman.io). This policy covers the desktop game on Steam.

What the game collects

When the game hits an error or crashes, it sends a report to Sentry, an error tracking service. A report contains:

  • Error messages and stack traces
  • Recent log messages and in-game actions leading up to the error
  • Native crash dumps, which may contain fragments of the game's memory
  • Game version, Electron version, operating system, CPU architecture, and basic hardware info

Reports do not include your name, email, Steam account or IP address.

The game collects nothing else. No analytics, ads or tracking.

Why

To find and fix bugs. Legal basis under GDPR: legitimate interest in keeping the game working.

Where it goes

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) stores reports in its EU region (Germany) and deletes them within 90 days. See Sentry's privacy policy.

Steam

Purchases, achievements, stats, and cloud saves are handled by Valve under the Steam Privacy Policy.

Saves and settings

Stored on your computer, and in Steam Cloud if you enable it.

Your rights

You can ask what data exists about you or ask for it to be deleted. Reports carry no personal identifier, so I cannot tell which reports are yours.

Changes

Changes are published here with a new date.
