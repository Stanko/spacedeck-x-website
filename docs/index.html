SpaceDeck

Build a deck under fire.

Dodge bullets, blast enemies and grab cards as you fly. Survive the action, then use your deck to fight a boss in a turn-based card battle. Get blown up, start again and keep fighting until you get your dog back!

A roguelike shoot-em-up deckbuilder nobody asked for!

SpaceDeck is a retro-inspired shoot 'em-up deckbuilding hybrid. I made SpaceDeck on my own and drew all of the pixel art by hand. The release is planned for the end of the year with the demo already available!

Highlights:

  • 3 player ships
  • 15 bosses
  • 70+ cards
  • 40+ upgrades
  • Infinite levels
  • 50+ Achievements
  • Hand-drawn pixel art cutscenes
  • Global and friends leaderboards
  • And maaaaaaaaaybe a local co-op
