SpaceDeck X

SpaceDeck X is a shoot-em-up deckbuilder where you draft cards in real time while dodging bullets and blasting enemies. Survive intense action stages, then face the boss in a card battle using the deck you built under fire. Get blown up, unlock upgrades, and don't stop until you get your dog back!

SpaceDeck X is a retro-inspired shoot-em-up deckbuilding hybrid. The release is planned for the end of the year with the demo coming to Steam this fall. It is developed by a single person (myself).

In the Steam version you can expect:

  • Achievements
  • Unlockable upgrades
  • Three player ships
  • More cards
  • More bosses
  • Story (told through pixel art comics)
  • Global and friend leaderboards
  • And maaaaaaaaaybe a local co-op

SpaceDeck X is also available on an arcade cabinet at Blast Galaxy in Amsterdam. Thanks to them and The Indie Arcade Amsterdam for the opportunity!

It plays really well on the arcade and if you are around be sure to check it out!
