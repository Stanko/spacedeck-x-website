SpaceDeck

SpaceDeck is a roguelike shoot 'em-up deckbuilder where you collect cards while dodging bullets and blasting enemies. Survive the action stage, then use the deck you built under fire to fight the boss in a turn-based card battle. Get blown up, start again, and keep fighting until you get your dog back!

SpaceDeck is a retro-inspired shoot 'em-up deckbuilding hybrid. The release is planned for the end of the year with the demo coming to Steam this fall. It is developed by a single person (myself).

In the Steam version you can expect:

  • 3 player ships
  • 15 bosses
  • 70+ cards
  • 40+ upgrades
  • Infinite levels
  • Achievements
  • Pixel Art Cutscenes
  • Global and friends leaderboards
  • And maaaaaaaaaybe a local co-op

SpaceDeck at the arcade

SpaceDeck is also available on an arcade cabinet at Blast Galaxy in Amsterdam. Thanks to them and The Indie Arcade Amsterdam for the opportunity!

It plays really well on the arcade and if you are around be sure to check it out!
